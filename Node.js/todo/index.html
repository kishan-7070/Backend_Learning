2028-Batch: 10-Hour Coding Marathon | Self-Learner Screening Assessment

Dear Students,

This is to inform you that a 10-Hour Coding Marathon will be conducted on 22 September 2026 (Tuesday) for all students who have nominated themselves for the Self-Learner Process.

This Coding Marathon will serve as a screening round for the Self-Learner Process. Students who qualify in the respective phases will be shortlisted for the subsequent rounds of the Self-Learner Process.

The Coding Marathon will be conducted in four phases, with each phase serving as an elimination round.

Details of the Four Phases

Phases Time No. of Coding Questions Level of Questions Topics
Coding Marathon Assessment 1 8:00 AM – 10:00 AM 8 Easy Loops & Patterns, Arrays & Strings
Coding Marathon Assessment 2 10:30 AM – 12:30 PM 6 2 Easy + 4 Medium Arrays & Strings, Linked List
Coding Marathon Assessment 3 1:15 PM – 4:15 PM 8 7 Medium + 1 Hard Arrays & Strings, Linked List, Stack, Queue, Tree
Coding Marathon Assessment 4 4:30 PM – 7:30 PM 3 Hard DP, Tree, Graph

Programming Languages

C / C++ / Java

Important Instructions

  • The assessment is applicable to students nominated for the Self-Learner Process.
  • Each phase will be an elimination round.
  • Only shortlisted students will proceed to the subsequent rounds of the Self-Learner Process.
  • The Seating Plan will be shared tomorrow, 21 September 2026.
